Coding rules

Source: Fakta om folkvalda – Riksdagen 2022–2026. Each member has a short biography: an introductory paragraph with offices in the Riksdag, an italic title and date of birth, then the sections Utbildning (education), Anställningar (employment), Kommunala uppdrag (municipal and regional offices), Uppdrag inom statliga myndigheter (state boards), Uppdrag inom förenings- och näringsliv (associations, business, party posts) and Skrifter i urval (publications). Code from the book first. Use other public sources only when the book is silent or unclear, and always say so in the comment.

Occupation

We follow the rule of the Global Legislators Database (GLD), so that our figures can be compared with theirs: the occupation is the member’s last full-time paid job that was neither an elected office nor a political job, held before the member’s first paid elected office. The aim is to capture the member’s social position before a political career could change it. Copy the job from the Anställningar section word for word, including employer and years, for example “Chaufför, Swedlog, Helsingborg 13–14”.

  1. Find the anchor: the year of the member’s first paid elected office. In Sweden these are kommunalråd, oppositionsråd, regionråd or landstingsråd, borgarråd, riksdagsledamot and member of the European Parliament, plus paid full-time elected posts in a party or trade union (for example partiordförande or förbundsordförande). Ordinary seats in kommunfullmäktige, regionfullmäktige, nämnder and boards are not paid offices (they carry a fee, not a salary) and do not count as the anchor.
  2. Use the Anställningar section to find the last job that began before the anchor year. Municipal, regional, state and association offices are not jobs and must not be used as the occupation.
  3. Skip political jobs, even though they are paid. These are appointments by a politician or a party group (politisk sekreterare, politiskt sakkunnig, statssekreterare, press secretary for a minister or party leader, political assistant to an MP or MEP) and all jobs in a party or party organisation, youth wings included (partisekreterare, partiombudsman, kommunikatör at a party office). If such a job is the last one before the anchor, go back to the job before it.
  4. Employment by a trade union is a job and is coded like any other (for example ombudsman or negotiator at a union); it matters for the working-class rule below. So are jobs in organisations outside the party, such as a study association, even when they are close to it.
  5. If the member entered the first paid elected office within three years of finishing their education, and the jobs before it were student jobs, summer jobs or part-time jobs alongside studies, write “student” as the occupation, leave ISCO and working class empty, and explain in the comment. If the member held a regular full-time job before the anchor, code that job even if it was short.
  6. If several jobs are listed without years, take the one mentioned last before the anchor. If the book lists a string of occupations without years (for example “Fastighetsförvaltare, säljare, restauratör, egen företagare”), copy the whole string and code the ISCO from the last or most specific one; explain in the comment.
  7. If there is no Anställningar section, or no job before the anchor can be identified, use the italic title if it is an occupation (for example “Lärare”, “Jurist”, “Undersköterska”) and write “occupation from title” in the comment. If the title is political (“Riksdagsledamot”, “Kommunalråd”, “Politisk sekreterare”) or missing, leave the occupation empty and write “no job stated” in the comment.
  8. Note in the comment if the chosen job ended more than ten years before the anchor, or if the member went straight from a political job into elected office.

ISCO-08

Give the four-digit ISCO-08 code. Look it up in Statistics Sweden’s index of occupational titles, which gives the Swedish SSYK 2012 code and the ISCO-08 code side by side: search the PDF for the Swedish title (Cmd+F or Ctrl+F) and copy the ISCO-08 column, not the SSYK column. For “Bankkassör”, for example, the index gives SSYK 3312 and ISCO-08 4211, so enter 4211: here even the first digit, which decides working class, differs. Many titles appear in several rows, one for each sector or level, with different codes; pick the row that fits the member’s job. If the book does not say enough to settle all four digits, give as many leading digits as you are sure of (three, two or one) and say why in the comment; the first worked example below shows how. If even the first digit is uncertain, give the likeliest code and explain. For the working-class rule only the first digit matters. If the title is not in the index, use CascotWeb, the tool the GLD used: translate the Swedish job title into English (truckförare → forklift driver, undersköterska → assistant nurse), type it in and take the best match. Note in the comment when the translation was uncertain.

Working class (1/0)

1 if the first digit of the ISCO code is 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9; otherwise 0. Exceptions, following the GLD:

  • A farmer or forest owner running their own farm or forest (major group 6) is 0. An employed farm worker is 1.
  • Police officers and military personnel (major group 0) are 0.
  • Employees of a trade union (ombudsman, negotiator, union officer) are 1 whatever the ISCO code.
  • A self-employed person whose trade is stated (for example “egen företagare, snickare” or “frisör, egen salong”) is coded by the trade (1); explain in the comment. “Egen företagare” without a trade gets ISCO 1 and working class 0.
  • A supervisor or manager in a working-class trade (for example “arbetsledare, bygg”, “butikschef”, “förman”) is 0 whatever the ISCO code; explain in the comment.
  • If the occupation is empty or “student”, leave working class empty.

The sheet flags rows where the working-class code departs from the first ISCO digit, until the comment explains why.

Comment and coder

Write a short note, in English, on every judgement that was not obvious: which office you used as the anchor, a choice between several jobs, political jobs you skipped, the student rule, an uncertain ISCO code, an ambiguous title, a source other than the book. Leave it empty when everything was clear. Be concrete (“chose X over Y because …”). The coder column holds the initials of the party member who coded the row; the sheet fills in yours when you code an empty row.

Kommunala uppdrag: kommunfullmäktige and nämnd seats from 2013 (unpaid), no kommunalråd post. Riksdag from 2021.

→ Occupation “Chaufför, Swedlog, Helsingborg 13–14”; ISCO 83; working class 1; comment “Anchor Riksdag 2021. Skipped politisk sekreterare 15–21 (political appointment). The book does not say what he drove, and the index codes a chaufför 8322 (car or van), 8331 (bus) or 8332 (lorry), so only the shared digits.”

Andersson Tay, Andrea (V), PDF page 15. Title “Humanekolog”. Anställningar: Utbildningsledare Jordens Vänner 15–16, Verksamhetsutvecklare Fältbiologerna 16–17, Politisk sekreterare V 17–22. Kommunala uppdrag: ersättare in a nämnd 21–22 (unpaid). Riksdag from 2022.

Anställningar: Servitris, McDonalds 04–06; Servitris, A-concept 06; Marknadsassistent, Netigate 06–07; Landstingsrådssekreterare 07–09; Administrativ assistent, Handelshögskolan 09; Politisk assistent, Europaparlamentet 09–10. Other public sources, in order of preference

  1. riksdagen.se, the member’s page (the same biography as the book, sometimes updated).
  2. SVT Valkompass 2022, where candidates describe their own occupation.
  3. The party’s or party district’s presentation of the member.
  4. Swedish Wikipedia, but only facts with a reference.
  5. Local press from the time the member was first elected.

Use only public sources. Do not use commercial people-search sites or LinkedIn. Write the source in the comment. If sources disagree, the book takes precedence; note the discrepancy.
